🛡️ Online Safety Tips

🔐 Never Share OTP

Never share your OTP, password or banking PIN with anyone.

🔗 Check Suspicious Links

Avoid clicking unknown or suspicious links. Always check the website address carefully.

🚨 Beware of Urgent Messages

Scammers often create urgency to make you act quickly without thinking.

💰 Beware of Fake Prizes

Be careful with messages claiming you won money, prizes or rewards.

🏦 Verify Banking Messages

Contact your bank directly if you receive suspicious banking messages.
